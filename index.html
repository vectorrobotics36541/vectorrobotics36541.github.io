Mission

Two components, one team.

In physics, a vector needs both a size and a direction to mean anything. We built the team the same way — the discipline to build well, aimed at a reason worth building for.

MAGNITUDE

Engineering, done rigorously

CAD before fabrication. Test logs before competition. We treat our robot like an engineering project, not a guess — documenting iterations, failures included, in a real engineering notebook.

DIRECTION

STEM, pointed outward

We compete to get better at building things, but we exist to bring more people into STEM — through outreach, mentorship for younger teams, and a program built to outlast any one season.
